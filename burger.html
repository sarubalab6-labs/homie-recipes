Juicy Homemade Burger 🍔

Ingredients

  • Burger buns
  • Veggie or chicken patty
  • Cheese
  • Onion
  • Tomato
  • Lettuce
  • Mayonnaise

How to Make

  1. Prepare and cook the burger patty.
  2. Toast the burger buns lightly.
  3. Spread mayonnaise on the buns.
  4. Add lettuce, tomato and onion.
  5. Place the patty and cheese on top.
  6. Close the burger and serve hot!
