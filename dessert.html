Sweet Homemade Dessert 🍰

Ingredients

  • Milk
  • Sugar
  • Fresh cream
  • Chocolate
  • Vanilla essence
  • Fruits

How to Make

  1. Heat the milk gently.
  2. Add sugar and mix well.
  3. Add fresh cream and chocolate.
  4. Mix until smooth and creamy.
  5. Add vanilla essence.
  6. Chill and serve with fresh fruits.
