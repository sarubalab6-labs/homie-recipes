Appetizer Recipe

Ingredients

  • Vegetables
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Oil
  • Spices

Preparation

  1. Wash and prepare the vegetables.
  2. Heat oil in a pan.
  3. Add the vegetables and spices.
  4. Cook until soft and crispy.
  5. Serve hot.
