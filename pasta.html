Creamy Homemade Pasta 🍝

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Butter
  • Garlic
  • Fresh cream
  • Cheese
  • Italian herbs
  • Salt and pepper

How to Make

  1. Boil the pasta until soft.
  2. Heat butter and sauté garlic.
  3. Add fresh cream and cheese.
  4. Add the cooked pasta and mix well.
  5. Add herbs, salt and pepper.
  6. Serve hot and enjoy!
