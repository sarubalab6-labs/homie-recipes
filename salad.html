Fresh Healthy Salad 🥗

Ingredients

  • Cucumber
  • Tomato
  • Carrot
  • Onion
  • Lettuce
  • Lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper

How to Make

  1. Wash all the vegetables.
  2. Cut the vegetables into small pieces.
  3. Add them to a large bowl.
  4. Add lemon juice, salt and pepper.
  5. Mix everything well.
  6. Serve fresh and enjoy!
