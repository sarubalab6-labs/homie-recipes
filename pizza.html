Delicious Homemade Pizza 🍕

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Onion
  • Capsicum
  • Olives
  • Italian herbs

How to Make

  1. Prepare the pizza dough.
  2. Spread tomato sauce over the dough.
  3. Add cheese and your favorite toppings.
  4. Bake until the cheese melts and becomes golden.
  5. Slice and serve hot.
